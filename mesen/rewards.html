What the fly wants

What the fly is rewarded and punished for, the way food and pain teach an animal. Events are detected the same way in every game from the score and lives in RAM and the objects on screen. Set a weight to 0 to switch an event off, negative to punish it. Changes apply to the running fly at once and are saved for this game.

How strongly each sense drives the brain

Gain × the calibrated level, for this game. Above 1 the fly reacts to that sense more readily (jumpier), below 1 it pays it less attention, 0 shuts it off.

Taste it

Let rewards stimulate sweet-taste (LB3, LB2d) and reward dopamine (PAM) neurons, punishments bitter-taste (LB1) and punishment dopamine (PPL1), as food and pain do in a real fly. The brain has no plasticity here, so this changes what it does in the moment; the learning itself stays in the learner.
